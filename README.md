# Universal Resume AI - Intelligent Interview & 22-Architecture Resume Builder

A modern, universal resume platform that interviews job seekers step-by-step—beginning from profile photo (PFP) and global photo etiquette down to quantifiable metrics, education, and keywords—and dynamically matches them with the highest-converting resume architecture across **22 specialized layouts** and **dual page orientations (Portrait & Landscape)**.

Created & Developed by [**Great_mujey**](https://x.com/Great_mujey) (Connect on X: [@Great_mujey](https://x.com/Great_mujey)).

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Interactive Career Interview Wizard
- **Step-by-Step Guided Interview**: Acts as an AI Career Consultant.
- **Phase 1: Profile Picture (PFP) & Regional Etiquette**:
  - Educational guidance on ATS & regional norms (e.g., US/UK anti-bias compliance vs. European/Asian dossier customs).
  - Photo upload, shape framing (Circle, Rounded, Square), visibility toggle, or sample avatar picker.
- **Phase 2: Contact & Identity**: Full name, title, email, phone, location, LinkedIn, GitHub, website.
- **Phase 3: Format Determination Engine**:
  - Dynamically evaluates user's target industry, seniority level (Student to C-Suite), and target region.
  - Generates a Match Confidence score and itemized justification for why a specific layout will succeed.
- **Phase 4: Executive Elevator Pitch**:
  - AI prompt presets: *Impact-Driven*, *Leadership & Exec*, *Tech & Architect*, and *Early Career*.
- **Phase 5: Employment History & Accomplishments**:
  - Google XYZ Formula methodology (*Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]*).
  - High-impact power verbs list (`Spearheaded`, `Architected`, `Optimized`, `Scaled`, etc.).
- **Phase 6: Skill Clusters & Keywords**: Grouped categories (Languages, Frameworks, Cloud, Leadership, Tools).
- **Phase 7: Education & Academics**: Degrees, institutions, honors, graduation dates, and coursework.
- **Phase 8: Featured Projects**: Project name, tech stack, deployed URL, and impact description.
- **Phase 9: Certifications & Languages**: Credentials (AWS, PMP, CFA) and multilingual CEFR ratings.
- **Phase 10: Suitability Report**: Summary audit and confetti celebration.

### 2. Universal Resume Architectures (22 Complete Layouts)
1. **Modern ATS Pro**: Clean single-column, 100% parser safe across Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo.
2. **Executive Ivy / Wall Street**: Classic Harvard/Wharton serif standard for VP, Directors, Finance, and Consulting.
3. **Creative Studio & Dynamic**: Asymmetrical two-column sidebar layout highlighting aesthetics, visual portfolio, and branding.
4. **Minimalist Nordic**: Light lines, breathable whitespace, modern typography; ideal for entry-level and career switchers.
5. **Tech Terminal / Silicon**: Monospace touches, code tags, and project-first layout for DevOps and Backend engineers.
6. **International Europass / Compact**: Structured 2-column with photo badge and CEFR language ratings for European and global markets.
7. **Compact One-Pager**: Ultra-dense single-page grid, micro-spacing, horizontal skill pills, compact timeline.
8. **Stanford Academic CV**: Classical Latinate CV for Faculty, Research Scientists, and PhD Fellows.
9. **Infographic & Metrics**: High-impact KPI stat cards, progress meters, and visual progression callouts.
10. **Elegant Vogue / Editorial**: Haute editorial layout, delicate serif, generous border for luxury and design roles.
11. **Silicon Valley Startup**: YC founder & early engineer framing, "Shipped & Impact" tags, open-source focus.
12. **Medical & Clinical Pro**: Board certifications, state licensure, hospital hierarchy, and clinical rotations.
13. **Legal Juris Doctor**: Bar admissions, trial litigation matters, stately serif standard for attorneys and general counsel.
14. **Hybrid Functional**: Skills-centric competency domains with chronological backing for career pivots.
15. **Dark Mode Executive**: Slate charcoal canvas, luminous accents, high contrast for modern digital leaders and VCs.
16. **Bold Headline / Marketing**: Full-width hero header with high-impact value proposition for CMOs and sales leaders.
17. **Sidebar Accent**: Colored vertical pillar for quick facts with generous body room for detailed achievements.
18. **Timeline Journey**: Connected vertical chronological track with milestone nodes and career trajectory.
19. **Grid Portfolio**: Projects-first architecture with card grid, stack badges, and live demo links.
20. **Clean Corporate**: Fortune 500 enterprise traditional, structured horizontal dividers, board-approved.
21. **Swiss International**: Bauhaus-inspired typographic grid, asymmetric numerical index ([01], [02], [03]).
22. **Landscape Executive Slide**: Native 16:9 widescreen presentation slide layout for executive pitch decks.

---

### 3. Dual Page Orientations & Design Gallery
- **Portrait & Landscape Modes**: 1-click toggle between standard vertical (A4/Letter) and widescreen slide presentation formats.
- **Design Gallery Modal**: Filter all 22 layouts by category (`ATS-Optimized`, `Executive`, `Tech`, `Creative`, `Specialized`, `Academic`, `Landscape Ready`) with instant search.
- **ATS Compatibility Audit**: Real-time score calculator (0-100%) grading metrics, keyword density, contact completeness, and action verbs.
- **Theme Palettes & Typography**: 8 color themes and 5 typography pairings.
- **Print & PDF Export**: Dynamic `@page { size: portrait/landscape; }` injection for pristine PDF printing.
- **100% Client-Side Privacy**: Auto-saves to `localStorage` with zero server tracking and full JSON import/export.
